# THE SKY WAS NEVER REAL

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-selected: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, React Three Fiber, Three.js, Drei, GSAP/ScrollTrigger, Tailwind CSS, postprocessing and original GLSL.

## Product Purpose

A playable cinematic artwork: an ascension through a world that was never meant to be understood. Success is a continuous, reversible spatial journey from wonder through existential dread to revelation.

## Users

Visitors exploring an experimental artwork in a desktop or mobile browser. No account or installation is required.

## Positioning

The page begins at its native bottom. Scrolling upward physically climbs through five layers of an artificial universe.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five chapters: The Garden Below, The Forgotten Sanctuary, The Veil, The Corruption, Beyond Creation. One persistent canvas, deterministic procedural geometry, no game assets, no backend, no credentials. Sound starts muted. All transitions must reverse. Native touch, keyboard, chapter navigation and an accessible narrative are required.

## Brand Commitments

Title: THE SKY WAS NEVER REAL. Tagline: An ascension through a world that was never meant to be understood. Thesis: The closer we get to the heavens, the less real the world becomes. Final line: You were never beneath the sky. You were inside it. Original environments; references inform themes only.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's detailed creative and technical brief. No pre-existing source code, external visual assets or audio. Procedural world and synthesized optional ambience are implementation decisions under the user's autonomous creative authorization.

## Product Principles

- Let the world carry the narrative.
- Preserve upward progress and coherent descent.
- Give each environment a focal point and negative space.
- Measure performance; do not invent hardware guarantees.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced motion, readable contrast, keyboard and touch controls, a narrative alternative, WebGL fallback, and a way to skip intense chapters. No rapid flashing.
